## 1. Formats working now

General 2026 signal: carousels earn the most saves (Metricool reports 9x more saves than single images, from a 2026 study of about 24M posts). Reels still drive discovery, but engagement is softening: Socialinsider, as cited by a vendor, puts it at 0.50% in Q1 2026 and 0.48% in Q2 2026. Audio trends fade in about 7–10 days, while format trends last about 3–4 weeks. Sources: https://metricool.com/instagram-research-study/ and https://klap.app/blog/instagram-reels-optimization (2026, **[snippet only]**). Suggested travel formats include budget breakdowns, step-by-step guides, before/after and destination comparisons (https://skedsocial.com/blog/travel-brands-on-instagram.md, 2026, **[snippet only]**).

| Format | Why it works | How to do it with trip footage |
|---|---|---|
| **"X things I wish I knew before Puerto Rico" Reel or carousel** | Answers questions people search before a trip, so it gets saved and shared. "Essential tips before visiting PR" articles are a common genre (https://islaguru.com/articles/essential-tips-for-puerto-rico, undated) | Use 5–7 clips with a text overlay on each, for example: no passport for US citizens, toll roads that may not take cash, Culebra ferry sells out, bio bay tours depend on the moon, and parking in Old San Juan. All of these are sourced in section 6. |
| **Cost breakdown carousel** | High save rate (Metricool, above) | Slide 1 is a bold total ("5 days in PR: $___"). The next slides cover flights, stay, car, ferry, tours and food, each with a photo. Use the real receipts from this trip. |
| **Hidden-gem or food-stop list** (Piñones kioskos, La Placita) | Gives viewers a concrete thing to do. Piñones is known for alcapurrias, bacalaítos and coconut water. La Placita is a produce market by day and a nightlife hub after dark (https://sheiswanderlust.com/destinations/the-caribbean-travel-guide/puerto-rico/san-juan/attractions/pinones/ and .../la-placita-de-santurce/, undated) | Do one food close-up per slide with the price and spot name in the caption. For a Reel, go from day to night at La Placita. |
| **POV / day-in-the-life Reel** | Quick destination tours plus a strong first-second hook are standard Reels advice (skedsocial, 2026) | "POV: your first day in Old San Juan." Show the forts, the blue cobblestones and Calle Fortaleza, then end with sunset. Old San Juan photo spots: https://beautahfulworld.com/most-instagrammable-spots-in-old-san-juan/ (undated). |
| **Map-route or itinerary carousel** | Itinerary content is easy to save and reshare | Draw a route from San Juan to El Yunque, the Ceiba ferry, Vieques/Culebra and Rincón, then use one slide per stop. |
| **"Expectation vs reality" / before-after** | Before/after is a recommended format (skedsocial, 2026) | Pair it with a moon or weather note, for example a bio bay on a bright night versus a new-moon night, or a rainy El Yunque versus a clear one. |

## 2. Topical hooks (now to late November 2026)

- **Hurricane season runs until Nov 30.** Risk drops off after the mid-Aug to late-Oct peak, and November is described as drier with highs in the low 80s °F. Angle: "Is PR worth it in hurricane season's tail?" Source: https://app.cohorted.co.uk/news/puerto-rico-in-november-weather (blog, undated; check NHC before posting).
- **Bio bays and the moon.** The glow is best near a new moon. Mosquito Bay (Vieques) is often called the brightest. Swimming is allowed only at La Parguera (https://welcome.topuertorico.org/biobays.shtml, undated; https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/index.php/profile/la-parguera-bioluminescent-bay, undated). New moons fall around **Oct 10–11** and **Nov 9, 2026**, though sources differ by time zone (https://calendar-12.com/moon_phases/2026 and https://catalina.lpl.arizona.edu/moon-phases/month/2026-10, accessed 2026-10-11). This is a good time to post bio bay footage.
- **Rincón surf season starting.** Peak surf is roughly Oct–Apr. November is a quieter month with variable surf. Whales are most reliable Jan–Mar, so don't promise them in November (https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/seasons-puerto-rico and https://www.mariasbeach.com/winter-whale-watching-in-rincon-pr/, undated).
- **Veterans Day, Wed Nov 11. Discovery of Puerto Rico Day, Thu Nov 19.** Nov 19 is traditionally treated as the start of PR's Christmas season. **Thanksgiving is Thu Nov 26** (https://www.timeanddate.com/holidays/puerto-rico/ and https://www.sanjuancruiseport.com/destination/events/, accessed 2026-10-11, **[snippet only]**).
- **Long Christmas season.** It runs from Thanksgiving to mid-January, with parrandas and lechón, and La Ruta del Lechón is in Cayey (https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/index.php/article/holiday-traditions-puerto-rico, undated). Angle: "PR does the longest Christmas," using any lechón or food footage.
- **Festival Lágrimas de Monte**, Nov 29, 2026, Hacienda Doña Fela, Hatillo (https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/event/festival-lagrimas-de-monte/2330, accessed 2026-10-11).
- **Unverified for 2026:** Festival Nacional del Plátano (Corozal) and Festival Mágico de Luces (Bayamón). Only 2025 listings were found (https://www.plateapr.com/en/things-to-do/november-events-in-puerto-rico-things-to-do-and-where-to-go).

## 4. Hashtags & keywords

Instagram reportedly now **caps hashtags at 5 per post**, announced December 2025 with some sources saying June 2026. Captions, keywords and spoken audio matter more for discovery as a result (https://later.com/blog/instagram-hashtags/amp and https://www.inro.social/tools/instagram-hashtag-generator, 2026, **[snippet only]**). **Use 3–5 tags per post.**

- Destination tags: #puertorico #oldsanjuan #elyunque #vieques #culebra #rinconpuertorico
- Niche tags: #caribbeantravel #puertoricotravel #biobay #puertoricofood
- Caption keywords (write them naturally, and add them to alt text too): "Puerto Rico travel tips", "things to do in Old San Juan", "El Yunque rainforest hike", "bioluminescent bay Vieques", "ferry to Culebra", "Puerto Rico itinerary 5 days", "Piñones food", "La Placita Santurce nightlife", "Puerto Rico in November"

## 5. Audio

No reliable source lists the current top travel audio, and audio trends turn over in about 7–10 days (klap.app, 2026). **Check the Reels audio browser before posting.** Look for tracks marked with the trending arrow and a low but rising use count. Good fits for this trip are reggaetón or Latin pop for nightlife and food clips, and calm instrumental or original sound with a voiceover for tips and bio bay content. Voiceover tip content works without trending audio and keeps working after a trend fades.

## 6. Practical facts travellers search for (good for "wish I knew" content)

- **Passport:** not needed for US citizens flying from the mainland, with no immigration or customs. A REAL ID-compliant photo ID is needed (https://welcome.topuertorico.org/passports.shtml, undated; https://www.kayak.com/news/passport-for-puerto-rico/, undated).
- **Currency:** USD, and cards are accepted nearly everywhere. Roadside stands may want cash (https://www.discoverpuertorico.com/index.php/transportation/car-rental, undated).
- **Car rental:** A state US license works and renters must be 25+. Check that your insurance covers PR. AutoExpreso tolls may have no cash option. Park in a garage in Old San Juan (same source; https://blog.autoslash.com/car-rental-in-puerto-rico/, updated 2025-12-31).
- **Ferry:** Both routes leave from the Ceiba terminal, about 30–45 min to Vieques and 40–60 min to Culebra. Advance sales only cover published schedules, and Culebra often sells out. Fares and timetables found online are old, so check puertoricoferry.com or the app (https://vieques.com/vieques-ferry/, 2025; https://newsismybusiness.com/es/confirmed-only-current-month-puerto-rico-ferry-tickets-available, undated).
- **El Yunque:** The FS FAQ says no reservation is needed for the PR-191 corridor, but **La Mina trail/falls has been listed as closed for construction**. The date is unclear, so verify before posting (https://www.fs.usda.gov/r08/elyunque/about-area/faqs; https://newsismybusiness.com/reservations-no-longer-needed-to-visit-el-yunque-rainforest, undated).
