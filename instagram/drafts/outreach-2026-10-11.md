# Outreach and engagement drafts: 2026-10-11 (Puerto Rico run)

These are drafts only. You send everything by hand. Don't use automation, bulk likes or follows, or copy-paste comments.

**Location rule:** your comments and DMs follow the same rule as your posts. Don't say where you are right now. Write "I was just at..." or "I went to..." about spots you've already left. Never write "I'm here right now."

## 1. Daily engagement routine (15–20 min, made for travel days)

Do it once a day during downtime, like breakfast, a ferry ride or the evening wind-down. Stick to the same window each day. If you post that day, schedule the routine so step 1 lands in the first hour after the post goes up.

| Min | Step | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | **Reply to your own comments and DMs** | Reply to every comment from the last 24h, and ask a follow-up question where it fits (for example, "Have you been, or is it on the list?"). If you posted today, do this within the first hour. Add any repeated questions to `instagram/faq.md`. |
| 5–12 | **Comment on 4–5 posts from the list below** | Comment on recent posts only (last 48h). Each comment should be 1–2 sentences that mention something specific in the post: a place, a price, a tip or a question. Rotate through the list, about 5 accounts a day, so you cover all of them every 3 days. |
| 12–15 | **Search one destination keyword** | Search a spot you've already left (for example "Piñones" or "Old San Juan"), open the Recent/Top Reels, and leave 2 genuine comments on small creators (under about 20K followers). Answer their questions with what you saw firsthand. |
| 15–18 | **Stories** | Reply to 3–5 Stories from accounts you engage with. Story replies go to DMs and build relationships faster than comments. Reshare any Story that tags you. |
| 18–20 | **Log** | Spend one line in your notes on who replied back and who might be a collab fit. Those people are your pitch candidates (section 3). |

Travel-day shortcuts:
- If you're offline most of the day, do just steps 1 and 2 (10 min).
- Save posts to an "Engage" collection while scrolling, then comment on them in one go later.
- Don't comment on 30 posts in a burst. A few real comments beat volume, and bursts can trigger Instagram's action limits.

## 3. Collab / DM pitches

Before sending, fill in every [placeholder] with something real from the recipient's recent posts. If you can't name a specific post or detail, don't send the pitch.

### Pitch A: small PR travel creator (Story swap or Collab post)

> Hi [Name]! I've been following your [specific post, e.g. "Reel on the Ceiba ferry"] and the [specific detail, e.g. "tip about booking Culebra early"] actually saved me some stress on my trip.
>
> I'm [your name], I post honest trip breakdowns: what things really cost and what I wish I'd known. I've been putting together a Puerto Rico series from my October trip.
>
> Idea: a **Collab post** on "[Local vs visitor: 5 things first-timers get wrong in PR]". You give the local take and I give the first-timer take, and it posts to both our grids. If that's too much, a simple **Story swap** works too: we each share the other's PR tips with a question sticker.
>
> No pressure at all. Happy to send a rough outline if you're interested!

(~110 words. Under 20K followers, a Story swap is the easier yes. Offer the Collab post as the bigger option.)

### Pitch B: local food spot or tour operator (feature, no freebies)

> Hi [Business name] team! I visited on [day/approx. date] and had the [specific dish / tour, e.g. "crab alcapurria" / "night kayak tour"]. [One specific detail, e.g. "the guide explaining why the bay glows brighter on dark nights" / "the line moved faster than I expected"].
>
> I run a small travel account focused on honest tips and real costs, and I'd like to include you in my **[Eat Here / Worth It?]** post about Puerto Rico. I paid for everything myself and I'm not asking for anything free. I'd just like to:
> - get the name, hours and prices right (is [price I paid] still current?)
> - tag your account correctly
> - and if you have a minute, share one tip you'd give first-time visitors, so I can quote you.
>
> I'll send you the link when it's live. ¡Gracias!

(Send this after you've left. Don't promise reach numbers. If they ever offer something free or paid in the future, the post needs Instagram's Paid partnership label and/or #ad, and the caption should say so.)

Spanish option for the opener: "¡Hola! Estuve de visita y probé [plato]. Me encantaría incluirlos en mi post sobre Puerto Rico. Pagué todo yo, no busco nada gratis, solo quiero confirmar precios y horarios y etiquetarlos bien."

## 4. Story ideas to post after leaving each spot

Post these the day after (or later) you leave a spot. They build curiosity before the full Reel or carousel and tell you what people want to see.

1. **"Guess the price" quiz** (food or tour spot)
   Use a close-up photo of the dish or tour gear with a Quiz sticker: "What did this plate cost me?" Give options like $4 / $9 / $15 / $22. The next day, reveal the answer and add "full Eat Here breakdown drops [day]". This feeds the Real Cost pillar.

2. **"Worth it or skip?" poll + countdown** (attraction, beach, bio bay, El Yunque)
   Use one teaser clip with no spoilers, and add a Poll sticker: "Worth the hype?" with Yes / Skip. Then a Countdown sticker to the Worth It? Reel's posting time, so people get notified when it goes live. In the Reel, reveal how your verdict compared with the poll.

3. **"Where was this?" quiz + question box** (scenic or street shot)
   Post a cropped shot with a Quiz sticker listing 3–4 PR places (for example Old San Juan / Rincón / Vieques / Ponce). Add a slide after it with a Question sticker: "What do you want to know before visiting?" Log the answers in `instagram/faq.md`. They're future Wish I Knew post ideas for the strategist.

Bonus format (any spot): an "A or B: what should I post first?" poll between two upcoming posts, for example "Piñones food" vs "Old San Juan POV". This tests interest before you spend time editing.
