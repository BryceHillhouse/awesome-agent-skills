## Caption

> POV: your first day in Old San Juan and you didn't plan a thing.
>
> Honestly the best way to do it. Here's how mine went: walk the blue cobblestone streets early before it gets busy, head out to El Morro for the views off the walls, swing by San Cristóbal, wander Calle Fortaleza, grab a snack, and end on the old city walls for sunset.
>
> Things to do in Old San Juan, plus what I'd tell a friend going:
> - Wear real shoes. The cobblestones and hills are no joke.
> - If you're driving, park in a garage and walk.
> - One $10 National Park Service ticket gets you into both El Morro and San Cristóbal for 24 hours. Card only, no cash. Kids under 16 are free.
> - Get there early. The streets are a completely different place at 8 am.
>
> Save this for your Puerto Rico trip.
