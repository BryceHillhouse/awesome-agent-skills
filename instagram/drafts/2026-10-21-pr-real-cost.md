## What to log while you're still there

Write these in your Notes app now (or snap receipts) while you remember. Total, date and a quick note is enough.

- [ ] **Trip basics:** number of days and nights, solo or how many people the costs are for (split per person or total?)
- [ ] **Flights:** total paid, bag fees, seat fees, how far ahead you booked
- [ ] **Stay:** total paid including taxes and cleaning/resort fees, number of nights, type (hotel / Airbnb), area
- [ ] **Rental car:** base price, insurance/coverage added, any fees at pickup/drop-off
- [ ] **Tolls:** what the rental company charges you (check the final bill after return; it may show up later)
- [ ] **Gas:** each fill-up
- [ ] **Parking:** Old San Juan garage and any other paid parking
- [ ] **Ubers/taxis:** each ride (screenshot the app history)
- [ ] **Ferry:** ticket price, number of people, booked ahead or at terminal, any car/luggage fees
- [ ] **Tours/activities:** each one (bio bay, snorkel, El Yunque, fort entry, etc.), plus deposits and tips
- [ ] **Food + drinks:** running daily total, plus the name and price of your best cheap meal and your priciest meal
- [ ] **Groceries/snacks/water**
- [ ] **Other:** souvenirs, beach gear, sunscreen, phone data, tips, any "didn't plan for this" costs
- [ ] **Cash vs card:** note anywhere that was cash-only (useful for a tip line)
- [ ] **Free stuff you'd recommend:** beaches, walks, viewpoints (good for slide 6)
- [ ] **Photos:** one per category (airport/plane, stay, car/road, ferry, tour, food, "other stuff", sunset). Take any that are missing before you leave.
