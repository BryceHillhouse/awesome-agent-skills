## Caption

> Puerto Rico travel tips: 7 things I wish I knew before my trip 🇵🇷
>
> 1. No passport needed if you're a US citizen flying from the mainland, but bring a REAL ID-compliant ID.
> 2. Renting a car? You have to be 25+, and check that your insurance actually covers PR.
> 3. Tolls are electronic (AutoExpreso) and cash lanes are rare. Rentals usually come with a toll tag billed to your contract, either a daily fee or per use plus a fee. Ask which one at pickup.
> 4. Old San Juan streets are tight. Just park in a garage.
> 5. Cards work almost everywhere, but the roadside stands may want cash.
> 6. Ferries to Vieques and Culebra both leave from Ceiba, and Culebra sells out a lot. Check the current schedule on the official site or app, not old blog posts.
> 7. Bio bays glow best near a new moon, and La Parguera is the only one you can swim in.
>
> Save this for your Puerto Rico trip.
