If the creator turns out to travel mainly to warm, no-passport places, or is food-obsessed, narrow to B or C after the PR run. That is a hypothesis to test against saves and follows-per-post.

## Content pillars

| # | Pillar | Format | Goal | Example (PR) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Wish I Knew**: practical tips and mistakes | Reel (5–7 clips, text overlay) or carousel | Reach + shares | "7 things I wish I knew before Puerto Rico" |
| 2 | **Real Cost**: budget breakdowns and itineraries | Carousel | Saves + follows | "5 days in PR: $___, every line item" |
| 3 | **Worth It?**: spot showcases, POV, expectation vs reality | Reel | Reach (discovery) | "POV: first day in Old San Juan", "El Yunque: rain vs sun" |
| 4 | **Eat Here**: food stops and local spots | Carousel (photos) + Stories | Saves + community (comments, "where should I eat?") | Piñones kioskos, La Placita day vs night |

Stories (no pillar needed) are for behind the scenes, polls ("next post: costs or food?"), question boxes and resharing each new post. They should run after leaving a spot, never live location.

## Cadence

- **Start (first 3 weeks, PR run): 2–3 feed posts a week**, alternating Reel and carousel, plus Stories 3–4 days a week. That's lighter than the default (3 Reels + 1–2 carousels) because the time budget is unknown and a consistent pace beats a big one that burns out.
- **Scale up once the time budget is confirmed:** 3 Reels + 1–2 carousels a week, with Stories most days.
- Post at the **same time of day** for the whole PR run (suggest 6–8 pm creator local time). Format is the only variable that changes, so the analyst can compare Reel and carousel cleanly. Test hook style or time of day in a later block.
- Hashtags: **3–5 per post** (the reported 2026 cap). Put the keywords in the caption and alt text, e.g. "Puerto Rico travel tips" or "things to do in Old San Juan".
- Check the Reels audio browser on posting day, because trends fade in about 7–10 days. Voiceover tip Reels don't need trending audio.
